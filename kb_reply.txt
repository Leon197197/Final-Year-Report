where can I borrow laptops in University of Birmingham?
You can borrow laptops in the Main Library and Barnes Library, see our information on borrowing laptops in the Main Library and the Barnes Library as follow:
Main Library: https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/libraries/our-libraries/main-library
Barnes Library: https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/libraries/our-libraries/barnes

Can I order alcohol delivery in compas?
Yes, You can order alcohol  eelivered by Food Fellows. Adding alcohol to your order is subject to specific days and times throughout the week. It is only available Mondays, Tuesdays and Fridays between 2pm and 4pm. Please note that it is also subject to time slot availability.

Who to contact for lost items?
Any lost or found property is held by Security for a period of six weeks. Lost or found items, including bicycles, can be reclaimed or reported to the Security Reception, Aston Webb Building.
Security Opening hours:
    Monday to Friday - 09:00 - 16:30
    Telephone - +44 (0)121 414 3114
After six weeks any unclaimed property is passed on to Acorns Children's Charity for re-sale.
Further information relating to campus safety and security can be found on the Security web pages.
